## Legend

### Symbols Used

- `┌─┐ └─┘` - Process boxes
- `│ ─` - Flow lines
- `▼` - Direction arrows
- `┼` - Decision points
- `╔═╗ ╚═╝` - Section headers

### Status Colors

- 🟢 **Green** = PRESENT (Student attended on time)
- 🔴 **Red** = ABSENT (Student did not attend)
- 🟠 **Orange** = LATE (Student arrived late)
- 🔵 **Blue** = EXCUSED (Valid excuse for absence)
